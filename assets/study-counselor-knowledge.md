### How to Apply to Erasmus Mundus Programs

1. **Check eligibility**: Bachelor degree, English proficiency (IELTS 6.5+), good academic record
2. **Prepare documents**: CV (Europass format), Motivation Letter, 2 Reference Letters, Transcripts, English proficiency proof
3. **Apply online**: Use the application link above for each program
4. **Scholarship deadline**: Usually January (for full scholarship)
5. **Self-funded deadline**: Usually March-April
6. **Results**: Typically announced March-May

### Erasmus Mundus Scholarship Benefits

- **Monthly allowance**: 1,400 EUR/month (for non-EU students)
- **Tuition fees**: Fully covered
- **Travel allowance**: Included
- **Insurance**: Health insurance covered
- **Duration**: 12-24 months (depending on program)
