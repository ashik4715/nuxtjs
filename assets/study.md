This document provides a comprehensive tracker and program breakdown for graduate programs (MSc, PhD, and Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters) outside Germany. It is formatted for direct reference and integration into a static application tracker platform (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages).

## 1. Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM Consortiums)

### 1.1 EMJM in Imaging (Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Imaging)

- **Consortium Universities:** Politecnico di Milano (Italy), Tampere University (Finland), Mid Sweden University (Sweden), Vilnius University (Lithuania)
- **Degree Level:** Joint Master of Science (MSc)
- **Country Focus:** Italy, Finland, Sweden, Lithuania
- **Funding & Scholarship Details:**
  - Fully funded via Erasmus Mundus Scholarship.
  - Monthly allowance: 1,400 EUR per month (up to 24 months).
  - Full tuition fee coverage (9,000 EUR per year for non-EU students waived).
  - Travel and installation allowance included.
- **Application Fee:** 0 EUR
- **Deadlines:**
  - Scholarship Consideration Round: January 9
  - Self-Funded Round: March 31 / April 30
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** High match for 3D reconstruction, RGB-D mapping, signal processing, and visual AI research. Direct tie-in with IEEE 3D mapping publications.
- **Required Documents:** Transcripts, CV (Europass format), Statement of Purpose, 2 Reference Letters, English Proficiency Proof (IELTS/TOEFL).

### 1.2 IPCVai (Image Processing, Computer Vision & Artificial Intelligence)

- **Consortium Universities:** University of Bordeaux (France), Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (Spain), Pázmány Péter Catholic University (Hungary)
- **Degree Level:** Multiple Master's Degrees (Joint Diploma)
- **Country Focus:** France, Spain, Hungary
- **Funding & Scholarship Details:**
  - Fully funded via Erasmus Mundus Scholarship.
  - Monthly stipend: 1,400 EUR per month.
  - Full fee coverage and insurance package.
- **Application Fee:** 0 EUR
- **Deadlines:**
  - Scholarship Round: January 15
  - Self-Funded Round: April 7
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Core computer vision, deep learning, OpenCV integration, and automated document analysis (OCR).
- **Required Documents:** Academic transcripts, Motivation Letter, CV, 2 Recommendation Letters, Passport, IELTS/TOEFL.

### 1.3 EMAI (Erasmus Mundus in Artificial Intelligence)

- **Consortium Universities:** Universitat Pompeu Fabra (Spain), Radboud University (Netherlands), Sapienza University of Rome (Italy), University of Ljubljana (Slovenia)
- **Degree Level:** Joint Master of Science in AI
- **Country Focus:** Spain, Netherlands, Italy, Slovenia
- **Funding & Scholarship Details:**
  - Erasmus Mundus Scholarship available (1,400 EUR per month + tuition waiver).
- **Application Fee:** 0 EUR
- **Deadlines:**
  - Scholarship Round: December 20
  - Fee-Waiver Consideration: March 18
  - Self-Funded Final Deadline: June 5
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Multimodal AI systems, LLM integrations (Google / OpenAI APIs), vision-language processing pipelines.
- **Required Documents:** Degree Certificate, Transcript, CV, SOP, 2 Reference Letters, Proof of English.

### 1.4 COSI (Computational Colour and Spectral Imaging)

- **Consortium Universities:** Université Jean Monnet (France), University of Granada (Spain), NTNU (Norway), University of Eastern Finland (Finland)
- **Degree Level:** Joint Master Degree
- **Country Focus:** France, Spain, Norway, Finland
- **Funding & Scholarship Details:**
  - Erasmus Mundus Scholarship (1,400 EUR per month living allowance + full tuition waiver).
- **Application Fee:** 0 EUR
- **Deadlines:**
  - Scholarship Application: January 15
  - Self-Funded Application: May 31
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Optical sensors, 3D color/spectral mapping, point cloud processing, depth sensor data fusion.
- **Required Documents:** Standard academic portfolio, SOP, CV, Academic References, Passport, Language Certificate.

## 2. Canadian Universities (Research MSc Thesis & PhD)

### 2.1 Memorial University of Newfoundland (MUN)

- **Department:** Department of Computer Science
- **Degree Options:** MSc in Computer Science (Thesis), PhD in Computer Science
- **Location:** St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada
- **Tuition & Cost Breakdown:**
  - Tuition (International): Approx. 1,734 CAD to 2,600 CAD per semester (MSc); 1,499 CAD per semester (PhD).
  - Minimum Guaranteed Funding: 20,000 CAD per year (MSc Thesis); 24,000 CAD per year (PhD) through RA, TA, and entrance stipends.
- **Application Fee:** 120 CAD
- **Deadlines:**
  - Fall Intake (September): December 1 (Recommended for full funding)
  - Winter Intake (January): April 1
  - Spring Intake (May - PhD only): August 1
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Computer Vision lab, 3D sensor fusion, human-computer interaction, assistive applications.

### 2.2 University of Alberta

- **Department:** Department of Computing Science
- **Degree Options:** MSc in Computing Science (Thesis), PhD
- **Location:** Edmonton, Alberta, Canada
- **Tuition & Cost Breakdown:**
  - Tuition & Administrative Fees: Approx. 9,000 CAD to 10,000 CAD per year.
  - Minimum Guaranteed Funding: 24,000 CAD to 28,000 CAD per year (fully covers tuition and provides living stipend).
- **Application Fee:** 135 CAD (Selective application fee waivers available through Department of Computing Science for high-performing international research candidates).
- **Deadlines:**
  - Fall Intake (Early Consideration): December 15
  - Fall Intake (Final Deadline): January 15
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** High-tier AI and Computer Vision group, medical image analysis, robotics and spatial perception.

### 2.3 University of Victoria (UVic)

- **Department:** Department of Computer Science
- **Degree Options:** MSc (Thesis), PhD
- **Location:** Victoria, British Columbia, Canada
- **Tuition & Cost Breakdown:**
  - Funding Range: 17,500 CAD to 23,000 CAD per year via Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA) and Teaching Assistantships (TA).
- **Application Fee:** 172 CAD (Fee waivers offered specifically for qualified prospective PhD applicants in STEM fields).
- **Deadlines:**
  - Fall Intake: December 15
  - Winter Intake: June 1
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Software engineering architecture, visual analytics, human-centered systems, computer graphics and vision.

## 3. Irish Universities & National Research Fellowships

### 3.1 University of Galway

- **Department:** School of Computer Science / Insight Centre for Data Analytics
- **Degree Options:** Structured PhD / Research MSc
- **Location:** Galway, Ireland
- **Funding Schemes & Costs:**
  - Application Fee: 35 EUR (Can be bypassed if applying directly to an advertised funded PhD project position).
  - Hardiman Research Scholarship: Full tuition fee waiver + 22,000 EUR annual tax-free stipend.
  - Irish Research Council (IRC) Government of Ireland Scholarship: Full tuition coverage + 22,000 EUR annual tax-free stipend + research expenses.
- **Deadlines:**
  - Irish Research Council (IRC) Application: Late October / Early November
  - Hardiman Scholarship Application: Mid-February
  - Funded PhD Vacancies: Rolling year-round.
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Strong HCI research, data analytics, vision processing, intelligent systems integration.

### 3.2 Trinity College Dublin (TCD)

- **Department:** School of Computer Science and Statistics
- **Degree Options:** MSc by Research, PhD
- **Location:** Dublin, Ireland
- **Funding Schemes & Costs:**
  - Provost's PhD Project Awards / SFI Centre Fellowships: Covers full EU/non-EU fees + 22,000 EUR to 25,000 EUR annual stipend.
  - Application Fee: 55 EUR.
- **Deadlines:**
  - Fall Intake: April 30 (General application); Individual SFI project positions operate on rolling deadlines.
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Computer graphics, vision and visualization, multimodal interaction, ambient intelligence.

### 3.3 Dublin City University (DCU)

- **Department:** School of Computing / Insight Centre
- **Degree Options:** PhD in Computer Science
- **Location:** Dublin, Ireland
- **Funding Schemes & Costs:**
  - University PhD Scholarships & Project Funding: Full fee waiver + 19,000 EUR to 22,000 EUR annual stipend.
- **Deadlines:**
  - Rolling admissions for funded project vacancies; IRC deadline in late October.
- **Relevancy & Background Match:** Media processing, OCR and NLP integration, agricultural technology applications.
